White Water Rafting

Our mission is to provide safe, thrilling, and unforgettable white water rafting adventures while preserving natural river environments and inspiring a love for the outdoors.

History

Founded in 2010, White Water Rafting began with two boats and a passion for river guides. Over the past decade, we have safely guided over 50,000 adventure seekers down class III-V rapids across the country.
